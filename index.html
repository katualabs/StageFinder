How it works

Three steps to your matatu

  1. Pick your road

    Every route leaves town along one of five main roads. Choose the one that heads towards your area.

  2. Find the stage

    Each route boards at a named CBD stage. Use the stage page to see the street and the landmarks around it.

  3. Check the fare

    Look for the route number on the windscreen, then confirm the fare with the conductor before you sit down.
